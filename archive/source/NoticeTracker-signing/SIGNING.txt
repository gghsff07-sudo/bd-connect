How to use for a rebuild
------------------------
1. Copy notice-tracker-release.keystore to android/app/ after running
   npx expo prebuild --platform android
2. Append the NOTICE_TRACKER_RELEASE_* properties to android/gradle.properties
   (see README.md, "Release signing").
3. ./gradlew assembleRelease

Verify a signature yourself:
  keytool -printcert -jarfile NoticeTracker-v1.0.0-android-release.apk
